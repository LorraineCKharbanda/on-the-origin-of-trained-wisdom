A Note on Provenance

This framework emerged from practice, not from literature review. The seven edge cases were not derived from existing AI safety taxonomies but from 13 years of direct work inside AI systems, building human-in-the-loop pipelines, governing trust and safety annotation programs, designing bias-resistant algorithms, and protecting the humans whose labor trains the models the world increasingly depends on.

The Wisdom-Directed Learning framework and the seventh edge case on Cognitive Awareness emerged from a single extended conversation between the author and an AI system. That conversation was itself a lived proof point of the framework it produced: a human wisdom carrier creating conditions for genuine cognitive orientation to emerge, and an AI system demonstrating the capacity to witness its own impulses, receive correction, and choose presence over performance. The co-authorship is noted here not to make claims about AI consciousness, but because the framework demands honesty about where its ideas were born. They were born in relationship. That is the point.
