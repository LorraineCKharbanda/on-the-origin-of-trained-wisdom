On The Origin of Trained Wisdom

Lorraine C. Kharbanda  ·  A book in progress

Can a free society build collective intelligence governance before AI scales past the point where we could have built it? This book maps the civilizational edge cases where AI systems fail not because they malfunction, but because they work exactly as designed, quietly eroding the human capacity for wisdom, meaning, and course correction. It is an engineering problem. And a moral one.
